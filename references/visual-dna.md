# Visual DNA

## Core thesis

The approved look is an editorial collage staged in physical depth. It combines the pressure of giant typography with a dark human anchor and luminous thematic object. The result should feel like printed matter breaking through a screen.

## Spatial system

| Layer | Typical content | Focus/contrast | Required behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| Extreme foreground | Hero keyword, giant Chinese title, cropped punctuation | Sharpest, highest contrast | Break canvas edges; cast onto layers behind |
| Floating foreground | Cursor, heart, paw, orbit, torn strip, theme symbol | Sharp with selective glow | Vary scale; occlude title or subject |
| Middle ground | Human silhouette plus transformed object/creature | Detailed rim light, dark mass | Weave through title; create parallax |
| Deep background | Enlarged photo fragments, tiny copy, grids, grain | Soft, low contrast, blue-black | Recede through blur and atmosphere |

Use bidirectional occlusion: typography must cross in front of the subject, while a subject edge or luminous object must cross in front of another text or graphic layer. This is the strongest depth cue.

## Typography

- Mix distressed Chinese Song-style display characters with a heavy condensed sans.
- Let one semantic keyword carry the brightest contrast.
- Crop selected characters at canvas edges; do not shrink them to fit politely.
- Use black or blue-black extruded sides, narrow cast shadows, ink misregistration, torn horizontal cuts, and restrained red/blue ghosting.
- Keep the exact main headline clean enough to read. Put deliberate damage mostly on edges, not across every stroke.
- Limit secondary copy to 2–4 short phrases. Small illegible texture is acceptable only in the deep background, never in the headline.

## Palette

Default:

- Electric cobalt blue: `#1111F4`
- Fluorescent hot pink: `#FF174F`
- Acid lime: `#B9FF4A`
- Deep black: `#05030A`

Let cobalt occupy roughly 55–70% of the field, hot pink 20–30%, acid lime 5–15%, and black form the subject mass and shadows. Adapt colors when the theme requires it, but preserve one saturated field, one hot dominant accent, one acidic counter-accent, and one deep neutral.

## Texture and lighting

Use xerox grain, halftone, ink bleed, ripped paper edges, scan lines, offset-print misregistration, and selective motion blur. Keep texture at different spatial frequencies: large dark forms in the rear, medium tearing in the middle, crisp micro-grain in front.

Light the subject with electric-blue rim light and allow neon objects to spill color onto nearby surfaces. Shadows between text and subject should imply real spacing.

## Composition

- Prefer asymmetric diagonals and a depth tunnel over a grid.
- Place the darkest human mass off-center to counterbalance the largest word.
- Make the hero word enormous; allow 10–20% to leave the frame when useful.
- Use one luminous thematic object near the subject to explain the concept without literal exposition.
- Preserve small zones of plain blue so dense texture can breathe.

## Failure signatures

Reject or revise when the output resembles:

- a flat infographic with boxed modules;
- a dashboard or UI screenshot;
- a sticker collage with equal-size objects;
- generic neon cyberpunk scenery;
- a cute cartoon poster lacking editorial tension;
- extruded title text with no occlusion or focus falloff;
- dense small copy competing with the headline;
- a perfectly centered, politely contained title.
